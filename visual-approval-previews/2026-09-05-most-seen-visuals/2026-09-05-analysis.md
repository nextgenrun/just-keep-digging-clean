# Most-seen visual review — 5 September 2026

Start with the persistent HUD. It offers the clearest visual gain across an entire run using the existing artwork. Then improve the separation between the mining target, player and decorative terrain. Apply the same interaction treatment across frequently visited surface services.

## Exposure and priority

These are exposure estimates from the game loop and rendered checkpoints. No player-session percentages were measured.

| Priority | Visual | Exposure | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Persistent HUD and objective | Throughout ordinary play | The 107.5 px objective card, stacked utility buttons and five locked sockets occupy useful lower-screen space. Use a 48 px objective strip, top utility row and an ability rail that grows as powers unlock. Preserve Campfire/key 6. |
| 2 | Player, dig face and terrain | Every movement and mining action | The small survivor competes with large, detailed roots and decorative seams. Reduce decorative emphasis while retaining terrain/resource art. Give the current target a material/HP cue. Preserve player geometry and darkness authority. |
| 3 | Surface service prompts | Each selling, upgrading or refilling loop | Floating text sits over detailed roofs and NPCs. Reuse an approved plate to separate identity from action. Bobo's example reads “BOBO'S SHOP” and “E · Browse abilities.” |
| 4 | Inventory, map and shops | Repeated short visits | Continue the spacing and text hierarchy after the core gameplay pass. This review does not include a fresh full-panel audit. |
| 5 | Main menu, talent reveals and rare spectacle | Short or occasional exposure | Retain the existing art for this pass. Improving these first would leave the persistent visual crowding in place. |

## Mockups

1. **Compact gameplay HUD:** 01-mining-current.png → 02-mining-compact-hud.png.
   - Move Wiki, World Map and Menu to one top row.
   - Replace the large lower-left goal card with a smaller strip under the core HUD.
   - Collapse the five actually locked slots. Keep the available Campfire action, its key 6 and quantity.
   - Keep GP in the core HUD; place mining damage beside the core stats.
   - Retain the money/Star, XP and inventory artwork.

2. **Mining focus:** 02-mining-compact-hud.png → 03-mining-focus.png.
   - Uses the HUD proposal as the baseline to isolate the additional visual change.
   - Multiplies selected decorative root/foreground/structure/expansion image alpha by 0.58.
   - Leaves base terrain, resource imagery and light-system configuration alone.
   - Reuses an approved plate for the dirt target and its actual remaining 30 HP.
   - A finished feature should show this only for an active target and fade it after targeting ends.

3. **Surface return:** 04-surface-current.png → 05-surface-proposed.png.
   - Applies the compact HUD to the actual town scene.
   - Uses an approved plate to distinguish Bobo's identity from the action.
   - The real objective changes from mastery to discovery; both fit the smaller strip.
   - Uses the current NPCs, survivor, buildings and background.

The recurring world art also deserves a later focused pass: compare the contrast and scale of giant roots/mushrooms against the survivor, and the abrupt edges between dug space and solid terrain. This review changes decorative emphasis; it does not redesign the terrain renderer or character.
