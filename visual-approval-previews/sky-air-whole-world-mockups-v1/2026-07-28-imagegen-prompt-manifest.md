# Whole Upper-World Sky Mockups v1 — ImageGen Prompt Manifest

**Generation mode:** Built-in ImageGen  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`  
**Runtime status:** Review only

## Shared constraints

- High-detail painterly 2D side-view environment concept for a Phaser game.
- Preserve the approved portal-platform silhouette and exactly four attached
  portal gates wherever a portal island is shown.
- Leave a broad, readable flight corridor.
- No player, HUD, text, labels, logo, or watermark.
- No baked sun, moon, planet, eclipse disc, complete distant ring, or round
  celestial light.
- No mirrored repetition and no hard rectangular background edge.
- Distant silhouettes must not look like traversable platforms.

## Board 1 — West / Town / Level 1

References:

1. Approved town panorama — style, mountain depth, forest and warm/cool
   lighting benchmark; its moon is explicitly excluded.
2. Moonless alpine cloud-sea direction — cloud scale and open-flight language.
3. Current full-world preview — relative sky emptiness and portal-island scale.
4. Approved Level 1 platform — exact platform identity and proportions.

Prompt intent: connect the unchanged town and lower mountain horizon into a
moonless western cloud sea, then build visual density gradually toward the
Level 1 portal island using restrained cyan wayfinding, cloudstone fragments,
small antennae, cables, crystal spires, and one broad open flight lane.

## Board 2 — Divider / Level 2 Arrival

References:

1. Moonless alpine direction — left-side continuity.
2. Moonless celestial-ruin direction — asymmetrical ruin material language
   only; its large broken ring is explicitly excluded.
3. Moonless stormbreak direction — controlled Level 2 atmosphere.
4. Approved Level 1 and Level 2 platforms — exact identity and relative
   contrast.

Prompt intent: show a calm neutral sky saddle around the central divider,
followed by a gradual cool-blue to indigo/iron/ember transition into the Level
2 portal island. Use sparse broken arches, hanging chains, vents, antennae, and
distant foundry silhouettes without a hard biome seam.

## Board 3 — Eastern Level 2 / Heavenblock Ascent

References:

1. Moonless stormbreak direction — eastern weather and clear-lane structure.
2. Lower Sky Cloud Reef clean master — lower ascent identity.
3. Angel Heavenblock clean master — ivory/gold middle-ascent identity.
4. Devil Eclipse clean master — dark chained material identity only; its
   baked eclipse body is explicitly replaced by an angular crimson rift.

Prompt intent: connect the Level 2 storm corridor to a vertically readable
three-stage ascent on the right: Cloud Reef low, Angel Heavenblock middle, and
Devil Scar high. Share one continuous atmosphere and cloud system across all
three cards, feather every local composition into the surrounding air, and
keep the central flight channel open.
