# Level One Biome Generated Role ImageGen Prompts V2

Generation mode: built-in ImageGen, one call per distinct asset.

## Shared production contract

Each output requested exactly one production-ready `1536x1024` 2D side-view
UNDERSTAR asset with genuine transparent RGBA negative space on every outer
edge. The shared style was painterly, high-detail premium dark fantasy, readable
at gameplay zoom. Every prompt prohibited full cavern scenes, continuous floors,
playable platforms, rectangular card edges, characters, creatures, ore,
resources, collectibles, UI, icons, text, logos, watermarks, checkerboards,
green screens, borders, contact sheets, and multiple variants.

## Role clauses

- `background`: a broad, laterally spread, lower-contrast shallow relief with
  layered gaps, atmospheric edge light, tapered ends, and roughly 45-65%
  occupied composition.
- `ground`: a dense broken terrain-edge wedge with a lower mass, climbing side
  spur, torn openings, mergeable edges, and roughly 40-60% occupied
  composition.
- `foreground`: one compact, crisp, deeply shadowed decorative cluster with a
  narrow vertical or diagonal silhouette, long irregular tips, generous
  negative space, and roughly 20-38% occupied composition.
