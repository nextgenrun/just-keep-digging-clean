# Underground Star Tile Redesign V1 — ImageGen Prompt Manifest

## Generation route

- Built-in ImageGen
- Use case: `stylized-concept`
- Output: five landscape 3:2 review boards
- Inputs:
  - current `sky-stars-beauty-v1.png` atlas as color-order and footprint
    reference only
  - current constellation-star gameplay screenshot as underground
    material/scale reference only

## Shared brief

Create a professional near-black concept board with six enlarged underground
Star Block candidates in a precise 3-by-2 arrangement. Use the fixed runtime
order cyan, lavender, gold, orange, turquoise, violet. Add a narrow bottom strip
showing all six much smaller inside dark underground soil. The family must read
at roughly 32 px, fit one square tile, remain physically mineable and grounded,
and give all six rarities individually authored silhouettes rather than
recoloring one master.

Completely replace the current repeated five-point-star-on-rock language. Use
premium painterly-realistic 2D game art, frontal orthographic presentation,
controlled self-emission, and crisp value grouping. Avoid text, labels, UI,
characters, logos, watermarks, pixel art, cartoon styling, neon square borders,
giant halos, or loose effects leaving the tile footprint.

## Direction 1 — Starheart Geodes

Natural celestial geodes: irregular dark-earth mineral shells split around
dense luminous star-hearts, radial crystal seams, pearly mineral edges, and
trapped cosmic dust. The heart is physically embedded rather than painted onto
the surface. Every rarity receives a different cavity, crystal structure, and
rock silhouette.

## Direction 2 — Celestial Fossils

Ancient astronomical fossils trapped in stratified shale and basalt: eroded
radial anatomy, branching mineralized ribs, compressed stardust, hairline age
cracks, and metallic inclusions. The star identity comes from a mysterious
fossil structure and negative-space glow rather than a clean star icon. Every
rarity receives a different fossil pattern and slab profile.

Built-in output:
`call_mcrKf966y24QJqRPfKIU22Z8.png`

## Direction 3 — Astral Lockstones

Broken megalith mechanisms sealed into mineable blackstone: layered plates,
recessed star-map channels, partial concentric arcs, constellation pinholes,
central apertures, and mineral-filled fractures. Use missing segments and
weathered asymmetry so they read as archaeological artifacts rather than UI
medallions. Every rarity receives unique lock geometry and inlay.

Built-in output:
`call_ORPnnpAksqsxuVWzB356tzYM.png`

## Direction 4 — Living Starseeds

Rare living celestial mineral organisms: dark stone cocoons, petrified roots,
translucent mineral petals, and a glowing starseed suspended in a protected
cavity. Organic veins curl inward like roots feeding a cosmic embryo. Keep the
result beautiful, mineral, grounded, and non-grotesque. Every rarity receives a
different cocoon opening, root architecture, and seed form.

Built-in output:
`call_IJmKGRfIq3OxufKN6VcpioyW.png`

## Direction 5 — Eclipse Prisms

Dense blackglass meteor prisms containing tiny dimensional rifts: fractured
mirror facets, a dark central void with a razor-thin colored corona, prismatic
internal reflections, and thick orbital shard layers fused to the rock. The
star idea reads as compressed cosmic space and lensing light, never as a
literal five-point symbol. Every rarity receives a unique prism silhouette,
void, and fracture logic.
