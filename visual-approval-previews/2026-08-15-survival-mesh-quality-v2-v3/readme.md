# Survival mesh quality V2.1 versus V3

Review-only animated comparison. Production remains unchanged.

V2.1 is the motion-locked, full-glove Blender quality candidate. V3 preserves
its exact frames, alpha, registration and cadence while applying a conservative
final-pixel grade: stronger material colour, deeper local contrast and bounded
micro-sharpening. The grade does not resize, warp or interpolate animation.

The combined GIF covers all six approved families. Six additional GIFs play
each family at its configured native cadence so review is not biased by a slow
12 fps contact sheet.

Motion smoothing remains a separate approval lane. It must be evaluated
family-by-family against foot contact, pelvis continuity, facing, mining contact
and transition-size gates before any runtime frame count or timing can change.
