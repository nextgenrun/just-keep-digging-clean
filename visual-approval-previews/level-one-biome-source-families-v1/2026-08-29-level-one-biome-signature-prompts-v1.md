# Level One Biome Signature ImageGen Prompts V1

Generation mode: built-in ImageGen, one call per distinct asset.

## Shared production contract

Every final prompt requested exactly one production-ready 2D side-view
UNDERSTAR underground foreground formation at `1536x1024`, as a PNG with
genuine transparent RGBA background. The asset had to be painterly,
high-detail, readable at gameplay zoom, asymmetric, organically feathered, and
surrounded by generous transparent negative space. Prompts prohibited a full
cavern scene, continuous floor, straight or playable platform, rectangular
card edge, characters, creatures, resource or collectible symbols, UI, icons,
text, logos, checkerboards, green screen, borders, contact sheets, and multiple
variants.

## Per-family final art directions

- `weathered-rootways`: ancient twisted root buttress lattice fused with
  rain-worn charcoal stone, broken timber braces, fine root hairs, muted loam,
  cold cobalt rim light, and restrained amber fungal pinlights; the formation
  enters from lower-left and curls toward center.
- `fungal-rainwells`: hanging rainwell canopy from upper-right, braided wet
  roots, porous charcoal loam, mycelial lace curtains, pale teal and muted amber
  bioluminescent fungi, and sculpted droplets.
- `sunken-orchard`: low crescent of fossilized orchard roots and rain-dark stone
  terraces, gnarled trunk remnants, embedded seed pods, drowned moss, silt, and
  a root arch reaching from the lower-right mass toward center.
- `timber-cisterns`: broken circular cistern ribs and waterlogged timber braces
  fused into dark stone, collapsed sluice ring, copper-stained pipes, damp
  runoff, and a blocky lower-center silhouette with an off-center hollow.
- `cobalt-aquifer`: serpentine water-carved cobalt shale channel, layered
  dark-blue stone, porous openings, cyan mineral flow lines, wet calcite lips,
  and a low wave sweeping from lower-left toward center-right.
- `sapphire-grotto`: tall off-center geode crown cracked open in dark sapphire
  stone, varied cobalt and pale-blue crystals, calcite shelves, soft internal
  glows, and incomplete radiating ribs.
- `glacial-waterveil`: broad hanging upper-left formation with translucent
  glacial fins, frozen water-veil curtains, icicle ribs, blue-white calcite,
  dark cobalt anchors, long tapers, and open gaps.
- `drowned-observatory`: broken astrolabe ring and tilted observatory gear in
  drowned cobalt masonry, wet stone, tarnished brass arcs, flooded lens
  fragments, cyan caustics, and a lower-right circular hollow.
- `amber-silt-fault`: diagonal sheared slab from upper-left toward lower-center,
  with broken ochre sediment layers, resin fractures, stone teeth, compact dust
  pockets, and one interrupted amber seam.
- `honeyglass-pocket`: compact asymmetric cluster of honeyglass cellular
  shelves and resin bubbles in dark ochre stone, translucent hexagonal
  cavities, soft golden light, drip edges, and transparent holes.
- `resin-archive`: tall left-side fossil-stone shelves encased in amber resin,
  eroded tablet-like strata with no writing, chained archival ribs, tiny
  geological insect impressions, resin curtains, ledges, and open holes.
- `fossil-sun-vault`: broken circular fossil-sun rib wheel in deep amber stone,
  radial calcified bones, resin-gold seams, weathered vault fragments, a dark
  hollow center, and an incomplete asymmetric ring.
- `mercury-fold`: folded liquid-silver schist and dark moonstone frozen into a
  low sweeping S-curve, mercury beads in creases, cool reflections, torn
  mineral edges, and transparent openings.
- `magnetic-needle-reef`: tall directional reef of magnetite needles, black
  iron stone, silver spear clusters bending toward an off-center field,
  suspended fragments, and restrained violet-blue sparks.
- `lunar-mint-galleries`: crescent silver masonry, non-textual circular reliefs,
  lunar gears, oxidized mint machinery, moon-metal carbonate, hanging chains,
  broken gallery ribs, steps, and a large central gap.
- `mirrorstone-convergence`: two broken mirrorstone arcs converging around an
  empty off-center aperture, dark silver rock, mercury seams, reflective
  shards, pale cyan/lavender reflections, irregular tips, and open gaps.
- `basalt-emberworks`: stacked basalt hex columns fused with ancient forge
  channels, interrupted ember conduits, iron clamps, soot-dark ribs, a zig-zag
  silhouette, open gaps, and restrained orange heat.
- `obsidian-caldera`: incomplete jagged caldera ring of black obsidian and
  basalt, glassy knife edges, dark hollow core, thin lava seams, cooling cracks,
  and one collapsed side of angular fragments.
- `lavawheel-necropolis`: broken lavawheel half-buried in black basalt crypt
  masonry, radial furnace spokes, scorched burial arches, chained stone ribs,
  molten seams, ash, and an off-center circular hollow without skull symbols.
- `shattered-furnace`: torn furnace mouth split into three refractory-brick
  masses, broken iron hoops and pressure pipes, clinker, hanging slag,
  ash-black stone, sparse deep-red heat, large gaps, and a violent silhouette.

Runtime derivatives add only a 28-pixel outer alpha falloff and WebP encoding;
they do not repaint or synthesize the generated formations.
