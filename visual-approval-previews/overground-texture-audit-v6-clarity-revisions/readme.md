# Overground resource clarity revisions V6

Review date: 2026-07-28

Status: review only. Nothing in this folder is registered, preloaded, or routed
into the game.

`00-v6-contact-sheet.png` is a lossless comparison assembled only from the six
ImageGen boards. Use the numbered full-resolution files for close inspection.

## Scope

- Copper: broad saturated copper loops with turquoise verdigris.
- Iron: heavy gunmetal ribs with rust-red hematite seams.
- Silver: a high-contrast liquid-mirror crescent ribbon.
- Gold: one bold gold vein with three large native-gold nodules.
- Ember Ore: a dark cinder shell holding a small cluster of bright ember cells.
- Obsidian: an asymmetrical shattered-eclipse glass silhouette with violet,
  magenta, and cyan fracture light.

Each ImageGen board places one repeated resource identity over multiple current
ground families and includes a small miner-scale example. The boards are
concept/context references, not tile atlases or runtime-ready cutouts.

## Explicitly unchanged

Stone, Bronze, Steel, Magma Crystal, and the approved multi-value GP
special-tile family remain outside this rejection/revision pass.
