## Shared prompt constraints

- Polished 16:9 in-world gameplay concept for a side-view Phaser game.
- Three ground destinations spaced about five tiles apart.
- Exact post-relic progression state: Cloud Reef active; Angel and Devil
  intentionally route-sealed.
- Painterly dark-fantasy materials physically integrated into the cobbles.
- Readable at gameplay camera scale with restrained emissive effects.
- No player, HUD, text, labels, arrows, diagram framing, or watermark.
- No baked moon, sun, planet, eclipse body, or repeated celestial disc.
- No flat HTML/vector circle, floating hoop, giant doorway, or modern sci-fi
  construction.

## Option A — Relic Dais

Prompt intent: design three low circular slate-and-brass altars embedded into
the road. Each has a recessed center and three clear relic sockets. The cyan
Cloud Reef altar emits a narrow aether updraft; the Angel and Devil plates use
closed destination-specific shutters with only faint dormant seams.

## Option B — Aether Well Gates

Prompt intent: raise each gate into a compact portal well composed of a shallow
basin, two short pylons, and an open broken semicircle. Improve readability
during movement without creating a full-height doorway or blocking the flight
lane. Keep the cyan route active and the gold/violet routes intentionally
sealed.

## Option C — Shared Sky Altar

Prompt intent: join three low destination plates to a small central
three-socket relic obelisk through visible stone-and-brass channels. Route
energy only to Cloud Reef so the artwork communicates the progression order
without text. Keep the installation low and traversal-safe despite its larger
horizontal footprint.

## Production interpretation after approval

The mockups are composition targets, not sprite sheets. A production pass
would separate:

- opaque gate bases;
- destination-specific inserts and sealed shutters;
- relic-socket state;
- additive emissive overlays;
- a cyan activation column and particles generated or animated by Phaser;
- contact decals used to blend the props into the authored surface.
