# ImageGen prompt manifest - underground backdrop enhancers V7

Date: 2026-07-29

The library was generated as 100 distinct native 1536 x 1024 ImageGen calls,
not palette-derived duplicates or procedural placeholders.

## Shared production prompt

Each call used this invariant contract, with the biome palette and named asset
concept changed per row below:

> Create one production-ready 2D game background enhancer overlay for a Phaser
> underground mining game. Wide landscape 3:2 composition, intended for
> 1536x1024 output. BIOME: [biome palette]. ASSET CONCEPT: [concept]. This is
> NOT a complete background painting: it is a sparse transparent overlay layer
> made only of isolated distant decorative shapes and subtle luminous accents.
> Keep at least 55 percent of the image pure empty background so an existing
> cave painting remains visible underneath. No ground floor, no platform, no
> playable terrain, no rectangular border, no vignette, no characters, no
> text, no UI, no solid wall filling the image. Painterly dark fantasy 2D
> concept art with crisp material-appropriate silhouettes and softly feathered
> atmospheric detail, compatible with the existing cinematic underground art.
> Put all art on one perfectly uniform pure chroma green #00FF00 background for
> later alpha removal. Absolutely no green color may appear in the artwork.
> Keep at least 72 pixels of uninterrupted pure green along all four outer edges
> so adjacent cards overlap seamlessly.

## Complete named concept matrix

The exact visual nouns in every stem were explicitly requested. Each biome uses
ten different composition families: ceiling crown, side arches, hanging
network, central halo, side silhouette, reflection ribbons, constellation,
diagonal ribs, vertical curtains, and depth aperture.

### Weathered Roots

Palette: damp ancient loam, charcoal shale, deep teal mist, sparse warm amber
sap light.

1. `weathered-roots-ceiling-root-crown` - enormous ceiling-root crown, varied
   hanging tendrils, amber seed pods, open middle and lower center.
2. `weathered-roots-twin-rootstone-arches` - broken root-and-stone arches from
   the two side edges, never meeting.
3. `weathered-roots-mycelial-lace-curtain` - branching mycelial lace curtains
   from both upper corners with irregular amber fungal bulbs.
4. `weathered-roots-amber-seed-orbit` - broken root orbit and floating amber
   seeds around a completely open center.
5. `weathered-roots-drowned-timber-silhouettes` - distant broken belfries and
   root-wrapped posts confined to the outer sides.
6. `weathered-roots-rootwater-ribbons` - translucent rootwater reflections in
   the lower side quadrants without a floor.
7. `weathered-roots-spore-constellation` - sparse amber spore clusters joined
   by nearly invisible mycelial threads.
8. `weathered-roots-fossil-root-ribs` - colossal fossil roots entering from
   opposite corners and breaking before center.
9. `weathered-roots-rain-thread-veils` - separated rain-thread and hair-root
   curtains with wide transparent gaps.
10. `weathered-roots-ancient-knot-aperture` - hollow root-knot iris with a fully
    open core, sap motes, and short tassels.
