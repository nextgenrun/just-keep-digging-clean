# Bobo shepherd alternatives V1

Three review-only ImageGen character directions for replacing Bobo's current
golden-dog merchant identity with a funnier, more immediately readable
shepherd-like silhouette.

- `01-crooked-shepherd.png` keeps the closest continuity with active Bobo:
  olive coat, red neckerchief, ability potions, coins, contracts, and a sly
  merchant expression. The crooked crystal staff supplies a strong idle prop.
- `02-potion-sniffing-sheepdog.png` pushes the comedy and friendliness furthest.
  Its clearest animation hook is a slow potion sniff, eyebrow lift, ear flick,
  and satisfied tail wag.
- `03-tiny-flock-marshal.png` has the strongest small-scale silhouette and the
  clearest personality contrast: a tiny shepherd acting like a grand commander.
  The raised finger, medals, ears, and oversized crook support salute, lecture,
  and tail-betrayal activity beats.
