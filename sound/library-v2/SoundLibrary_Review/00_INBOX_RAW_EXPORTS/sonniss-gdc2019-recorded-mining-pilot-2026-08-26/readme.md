# Sonniss recorded SFX source library

Review-only set of 118 licensed candidates mapped to authored UNDERSTAR sound
families. The first listening batch contains 31 user-approved sources: 27
direct recordings and 4 clearly labelled designed-library samples. The
87-source expansion adds 68 direct recordings and 19 labelled designed samples
across mechanisms/UI, machine metal, air/Flight, surface contacts, ice/glass,
wood/doors, weather/water, electrical, chain, and thermal/fire. Human listening
approved all 118 sources on 2026-08-27.

Open `index.html`, use the filters to review the additions, and choose **Export
ratings JSON**. The 256 kbps previews are transparent twelve-second listening
copies only. Source WAV files remain beside the page for detailed inspection.
No EQ, pitch-shifting, synthesis, AI generation, or new layering was applied.

Source approval is not runtime approval. Files marked **SLICE REQUIRED** are
long recordings, loops, sequences, or likely multi-event reels. Record exact
edit points and export isolated derivatives before considering them for a
one-shot. Every source remains `runtimeEligible: false` in this inbox.

The [official GameAudioGDC license](https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/) permits commercial game use and modification without attribution. It prohibits AI training or use. Nothing here is runtime-approved or runtime-wired.
