03 / THE PERSONALITY & THE RULES

A person, with room to breathe.
Ordinary life first.
Pay, sore knees, supper, bad employers. Warmth around the fire and Stars. A blunt swear after a real scare. His stranger memories surface occasionally, attached to something you can see.

One clear feeling per line. The default examples use only words you already accepted; these delivery notes are direction for a later recording pass.

Fresh events beat silence.
Common ore pickups can recur, sharing a 45-second gap. New trips get a fresh departure. No exact sentence repeats in a session. If its suitable pool is exhausted, that family rests.

Normal breathing room is 25 seconds. A skipped pickup does not use up its opportunity: a later, genuinely new pickup may qualify. Nothing waits in a stale queue.

One moment, one voice.
Cinematics, narration and NPC conversations reserve the channel, including gaps between their sentences. Event remarks come next; wandering thoughts come last.

No voice interrupts another. If an NPC requests speech mid-Leo sentence, finish it and hand over. Wait 1.2 seconds after other dialogue; a fresh cue must still be visible. Colliding rewards receive one reaction.

Danger stays clear.
An actionable warning can bypass ordinary chatter spacing, with its own 45-second gap. It still cannot interrupt speech. The visual warning always remains available.

Relief waits for safety. If Leo just warned you and the relief would be late, skip the joke. Urgent warnings start immediately while music eases down.

A thought, every so often.
After 27–33 active minutes, allow a two-minute window for safety, 20 seconds without other speech and 90 seconds since Leo spoke. A new event wins that opening.

Menus, pause and hidden tabs stop the clock. Missed windows are skipped, never accumulated. This is the only context-free family.

The music yields gently.
Ease to 24% of the player’s chosen music level over 0.9 seconds. Start a normal line during that descent. Hold through speech and a 0.45-second breath; return over 2.4 seconds.

Successive voices share the lowered bed. No dip for a rejected Leo attempt. Muted speech creates no caption or music dip; the player’s music preference remains the baseline.
