03 / BROADER, WITH A CLEAR CONNECTION

One real action. One fitting response.

Several ways to qualify.

A fresh ore award can happen in a new vein, during ordinary digging, after travel or after a break. A return counts after real work; it need not cross an arbitrary depth. Any successful paid upgrade can earn a reaction.

A pickup, changed HUD value or visible world result can confirm the action. They are alternatives, not a checklist that all has to happen at once.

The words still have to fit.

Iron means actual iron. “Paid” means a completed sale. “Power gone” means powered flight actually ran out. An expensive-sounding line still needs an expensive purchase, even though ordinary purchase lines do not.

Never stretch a specific sentence across unrelated situations. The nine newly written examples are clearly marked as drafts, awaiting your wording approval.

Room, without empty stretches.

Keep the proposed 25-second ordinary gap and eight remarks per five active minutes. Broaden the eligible actions instead of filling silence with unrelated chatter. Ore shares a 45-second gap; each other family has its stated repeat rule.

Rotate context-compatible alternatives; do not repeat the same sentence within a session. A family does not go silent because its first-ever discovery has already happened.

A current situation can last.

A pickup reaction must start promptly. Ongoing work, resting and just-regained safety can remain relevant for a short window while that situation is still true. A vanished floating label alone does not invalidate a still-obvious action.

When the player changes activity, leaves the object or enters danger, the opportunity ends. No delayed backlog.

Other voices keep their space.

NPC conversations, cinematics and narration retain the speech channel, including pauses between sentences. Leo never overwrites them. If another voice requests the channel mid-sentence, finish that short line, then hand over.

Music still eases down over 0.9 seconds and returns over 2.4 seconds. The preview remains entirely silent.

Mystery belongs somewhere.

Let everyday events reveal the tired worker, bad joker and reluctant caretaker. Recognition belongs to a visible Star or Titan. A campfire can earn warmth without requiring an upgrade.

Unprompted thoughts stay around 30 active minutes apart, with the existing safety and quiet conditions.
