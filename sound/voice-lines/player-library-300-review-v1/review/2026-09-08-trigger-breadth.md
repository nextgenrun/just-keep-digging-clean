# Broader Leo trigger mockup - 2026-09-08

Open trigger-breadth.html. This is a second, silent proposal; it does not replace the earlier trigger decisions or wire any game events.

## What changed

Twelve gameplay families now cover 37 situations. Ordinary work, travel, useful collection, flight, selling and failed purchases join progression and discoveries. The new model accepts a real action plus an appropriate visible cue: pickup feedback, a changed HUD value and a world result are alternative evidence, rather than simultaneous requirements.

The departure/return depth thresholds and purchase wallet-percentage threshold no longer gate generic departure, return and purchase lines. A noticeable haul can qualify before the final bag band. Specific lines still require specific facts: material identity, successful purchase, actual sale payout, genuine flight reserve loss, or an actionable hazard.

Twenty-eight examples retain accepted words, with their broader usage explicitly proposed for review. Nine new short scripts are marked unapproved drafts. All 37 situations carry their exact script text, emotion and wording status in exported proposal snapshots. Storage validity binds the family, every situation and its words, the original catalog hash and timing policy.

The earlier pasted page reports 19 yes and 5 no, and identifies T24 as yes, but does not map the other decisions. Its bytes are preserved in 2026-09-08-trigger-v1-pasted-review.txt. No unlisted votes are inferred or transferred.
