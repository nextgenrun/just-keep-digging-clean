    How he speaks

      Timing, NPC priority and gentle music fades

      The event earns the line

      One reaction to one visible moment. Recheck the scene before speaking; discard a stale line. Normally at least 90 seconds between player remarks, with at most four in ten minutes.

      Warnings must still be relevant now. A new pickup, unlock or return never waits behind a long conversation.

      Other voices get space

      Cinematics, narration and NPC dialogue reserve the speech channel. Player events and wandering thoughts cannot interrupt them. Only one voice plays at a time.

      If an NPC starts during a short player sentence, finish that sentence, then give the NPC the channel.

      A rare wandering thought

      Count 27–33 minutes of active gameplay. Pause that clock in menus, pause and hidden tabs. Speak only in safety, after a quiet gap. Event reactions take priority; missed opportunities never stack up.

      Music makes room gently

      Ease music down over about 0.9 seconds, hold it under the voice, then restore it over 2.4 seconds after a short breath. Consecutive voices share the lowered music bed.

      The player's music setting remains the baseline.
