    Welcome below

    What is UNDERSTAR?

    UNDERSTAR is an atmospheric 2D mining game about carving a single persistent descent. Break terrain, collect ores, return to the surface settlement, sell and upgrade, then dig deeper with better tools, stronger movement and growing mastery of Gem Power.

    Wiki scope: Playable now describes the current public beta. Development documents a gated or evolving system and is not a promise that it is available in the public build.

    Primary loop

    Descend, return, improve

    Mining creates routes and resources. Portals preserve useful return points. Town converts cargo into money and permanent power.

    Current world

    Level One: 0–2,000m

    Twenty visual biome identities span rootways, flooded grottos, amber faults, magnetic galleries and volcanic ruins.

    Learn one useful loop

    How to play UNDERSTAR: your first expedition

    Choose the guided start for a step-by-step first route. The objective strip tells you what to do next using your current key bindings.

    1. Move. Walk through the marked opening route with A/D.
    2. Dig. Aim down with S and hold F at the starter seam. Hold the primary mouse button over an adjacent tile if you prefer mouse digging.
    3. Use Flight. Follow the opening prompt, then hold Shift in the air and steer with WASD. Space performs a separate fixed jump.
    4. Use the first portal. Continue through the opening shaft, approach the marked portal, and press E. Activate it before leaving the tutorial route.
    5. Sell. Return to the Money Monster and sell the resources you actually mined.
    6. Buy an upgrade. Pick an available shop upgrade and make the purchase.
    7. Resume. Use the learned return route and continue your descent with the improved build.

    Skipping the guided start grants Flight access but skips its practice reserve. It does not give you sale cargo or a free shop purchase.

    Hints & quick answers

    In-game Hints sorts these topics for your current situation and displays your remapped keys. Here, use search or choose a topic; controls below are the defaults.

    Getting started

    How do I dig?

    Stand beside a solid tile, aim with A/D/W/S, and hold F. You can also hold the primary mouse button over a nearby tile. Tough blocks take several hits. Close menus before digging.

    Read the getting started guide →

    Movement

    How do I fly?

    After the opening gives you Flight, hold SHIFT in the air. Steer with A/D; W/S build vertical momentum. Release Flight to stop its GP drain. SPACE is a separate, fixed-height jump.

    Read the movement guide →

    Survival

    Low on Gem Power

    Land in a safe, lit place. Release SHIFT and Q, and stop powered movement so GP can recover. Lower the torch intensity if needed; in Hardcore, keep enough light to control stress. Return through an activated portal when you can afford its displayed cost.

    Read the survival guide →

    Survival

    Seeing in the dark

    Press T to toggle your torch. Click or wheel its HUD meter to adjust intensity. Brighter light uses more GP. In Hardcore, darkness raises stress, so find light before letting your reserve run out.

    Read the survival guide →

    Exploration

    Return without losing your route

    Approach a discovered portal and press E when its prompt appears. Activated portals preserve return routes. Back in town, Esc > General offers Resume Deepest Portal when a usable destination exists. Check its GP cost first.

    Read the exploration guide →

    Town

    Turn your haul into upgrades

    Visit the Money Monster and press E to sell your mined resources. Buy upgrades with the proceeds. Your bag picture shows how much you carry; it is not a hard inventory limit. Check I for exact holdings.

    Read the town guide →

    Progression

    Why is an upgrade locked?

    Read the requirement shown on the shop row. Upgrades can require player level, deepest depth, Relics, money, or materials. Buying an earlier rank does not bypass the next unlock requirement. Journey shows your progress.

    Read the progression guide →

    Progression

    Talent Points and Stars

    Open Esc > Stars. The trees open at player level 3. Level-earned Talent Points unlock new nodes; Star Points improve ranks on nodes you already own. Select a node to read its prerequisites and price, then use its action button.

    Read the progression guide →

    Progression

    Choose a Celestial power

    Esc > Stars contains Wayward Star, Hollow Sun, and Stellar Lance. Unlock a tree's ability node, then use its Celestial action-bar control. Read the displayed GP cost and cooldown. The base abilities and their later talent ranks are separate purchases.

    Read the progression guide →

    Town

    Ember and the Campfire

    Ember Ore adds Campfire charges. Return to the Campfire and press E to inspect its blessings, stored charges, and upgrades. Choose a blessing in the Campfire menu, then use its action-bar control to spend a charge.

    Read the town guide →

    Survival

    An earthquake is warning you

    Move out of the marked falling-rock area before impact. Walk or fly toward clear ground and keep an exit open. Once the fall ends, the rubble can be mined. Watch the actual warning markers, not just the screen shake.

    Read the survival guide →

    Survival

    Avoid the Graveborer Wurm

    Mining noise can draw a Graveborer Wurm. Watch its breach warning and move out of the marked path before it arrives. Preserve GP for an escape and avoid standing on the breach line.

    Read the survival guide →

    Survival

    Protect a Hardcore run

    Once Hardcore is armed, hazards and loss of GP can cost a revive or life. Light, nearby Stars, and the surface help recovery. Keep a powered escape route. Importing or restoring a backup does not rewind Hardcore lives.

    Read the survival guide →

    Break, collect, convert

    Your defining movement system

    Flight and Gem Power guide

    Hold Shift while airborne to engage powered Flight, then steer with A/D and W/S. Flight drains Gem Power (GP); capacity, regeneration, efficiency and speed can all be improved.

    Read the GP bar

    Flight, the torch and active abilities compete for the same pool. Leave a reserve for the return route instead of spending the final point at maximum depth.

    Regain control

    Release Shift to stop powered drain, find a safe ledge and allow regeneration. In Casual mode, reaching zero disables power but does not delete your save.

    Depth milestones add permanent GP capacity. The Gem Power merchant offers tank size, drain efficiency, regeneration and Flight-speed upgrades after their progression gates open.

    Make depth reusable

    Portals & caves

    A discovered underground gate can be paired with a sky gate. Activate it with E, then use the connection to return, sell and resume without replaying every mined corridor.

    Activated portals

    The game remembers active pairs and can identify the deepest or nearest useful route. Sky gate capacity is limited, so newer deep routes can replace shallower entries.

    Caves and discoveries

    Open chambers interrupt the tile grid with authored shapes, geodes, hazards, relic clues, special blocks and event anchors. Look before cutting the only safe ledge.

    Hardcore variants can attach money costs to portal travel and quick resume. The tutorial’s first route remains protected so the opening loop can be completed.

    The surface hub

    Town & economy

    Approach a merchant and press E. The shop shows costs, ownership, requirements and whether you are buying, crafting or selling.

    Money Monster

    Sells individual resources or full stacks. Sell All is an unlock; crafting-material warnings protect resources needed by an unbuilt Arc Core.

    Player Upgrades

    Agility, Strength, Quick Reflexes, Critical Strike, Heavy Punch and Lucky Collector improve the permanent mining body.

    Gear Merchant

    Pickaxes have flat damage and material affinities: Bronze, Iron, Steel, Mithril, Adamant, Rune and Dragon.

    Gem Power & Bobo

    One develops Flight and GP. Bobo owns irreversible save-rule choices such as Hardcore; read the confirmation carefully.

    Money and upgrades persist with the save. Prices scale for repeatable upgrades; one-time tools require both money and, at higher tiers, mined resources or player level.

    Spend power deliberately

    Abilities & equipment

    Quickslash · hold Q

    Speeds up the mining rhythm while consuming GP per paid action. Buy the base ability from Bobo; your upgrades change its effective cost and behavior. Release Q to return to normal digging.

    Thunder Strike · V

    Buy the base ability from Bobo, then use V for a powered area strike. Check the ability’s displayed GP cost and current upgrades before using it.

    Torch · T

    Trades GP for visibility and, in Hardcore, pressure relief. Higher intensity improves safety but drains faster.

    Pickaxes

    Do not compare only the headline damage. Each tier has favored materials, so a lower-looking value can still be the correct tool for its layer.

    Read the world around you

    Weather & events

    Weather and underground encounters change the atmosphere and the decisions you make during a run. Follow the live event prompt and keep a route back to safety.

    Earthquakes

    Leave the marked falling-rock area before impact. Walk or fly clear, then dig the rubble after the fall.

    Graveborer Wurm

    Mining noise can attract a breach. Watch the warning line and leave its path before the Wurm arrives.

    Shadow Miner

    A shadowy miner can appear during underground exploration. Its activity belongs to the mine; keep watching your own route and GP while observing it.

    Crystal Choir

    These encounters use their own prompts and objectives. Read the instructions at the discovery instead of treating every event as an ordinary ore deposit.

    Surface weather includes clear skies, drizzle, rain, storms, and snow. Shelter and depth affect how strongly the weather reaches you.

    Blackout Bloom: this event is active in the public build. Follow its light and harvest prompts. Money Monster Rush is disabled.

    One world, different stakes

    Modes & saves

    ModeLivesAt zero GPSave behavior
    CasualUnlimitedPower abilities stop; recover safelyPersistent progress

    Hardcore is permanent for that save once confirmed and armed. Stress rises in darkness, rapid descent and deep pressure; light, the surface and controlled movement reduce it. Unstuck has a cooldown and can sacrifice resources.

    Save safety: UNDERSTAR does not automatically delete an exhausted Hardcore save. Use the pause menu’s save controls before closing a session, and avoid clearing browser site data if you rely on local progress.

    Three trees, two currencies

    Celestial systems

    Open Esc → Stars or visit the Star Pillar. Celestial talent access begins at player level 3. These trees are part of the current game.

    Talent Points unlock nodes

    Player levels earn Talent Points. Spend them on new nodes, following the selected node’s prerequisites. A locked node explains what is missing.

    Stars improve owned talents

    Mining Star Blocks earns Star Points. Spend them to buy further ranks on talents you already own. The panel shows the next rank’s cost and effect; Stars do not replace the Talent Point needed to unlock a node.

    Wayward Star

    Launch a Star that ricochets through blocks. Its talents extend and change the route, speed, and return behavior.

    Hollow Sun

    A moving celestial power sends alternating pulse waves through nearby blocks. Its talents change the pulse and movement effects.

    Stellar Lance

    While active, your punches fire energy waves. A wave carries damage onward after breaking a block. Its talent ranks improve the lance, range, and follow-up effects.

    Activate an unlocked power

    Unlock its ability node, then use the Celestial action bar. Read its GP cost and cooldown before activating it; unlocking a power does not make its use free.

    Outside this beta: Level Two, Arc Cores, Omega Arc Cores and Heavenblocks remain unavailable in the public demo. They are not requirements for Level One’s talent trees or Campfire.

    Know where to look

    HUD & menus

    Esc → Hints

    The field guide puts advice for your current situation first: active hazards, low GP, darkness, unspent Talent Points, and the opening objective. Browse all pages whenever you need a reminder. Read in Wiki opens the same hint in a separate tab.

    I → Holdings and Codex

    Holdings shows what you carry. Resource Codex explains materials; Special Blocks covers unusual finds and their effects. Star Codex appears when its progression unlocks, grouping Star identities by rarity with pages and detailed previews.

    Esc → Stars, Journey, Titans

    Stars contains talent trees. Journey connects progress and goals. Titans holds the discovery archive. Some information depends on what this run has unlocked or discovered.

    Esc → Settings and Saves

    Settings controls key bindings, audio, and presentation. Saves contains manual save, JSON export, and import. Esc closes the top overlay and returns control to the game.

    Quick answers

    FAQ & troubleshooting

    Is UNDERSTAR on Steam?

    Yes. UNDERSTAR: Dig Below, Rise Above is Coming Soon on Steam. Open the store page and choose “Add to your wishlist” to follow the Windows PC release.

    Can I play UNDERSTAR now?

    Yes. The public browser beta is playable now and uses keyboard-first controls. The Steam version is not released yet, so wishlisting and playing the browser beta are separate actions.

    What kind of mining game is UNDERSTAR?

    UNDERSTAR is a single-player atmospheric 2D mining adventure with destructible terrain, persistent tunnels, resource selling, upgrades, underground biomes, Flight, rare discoveries and optional higher-stakes modes.

    The beta appears to be loading for a long time.

    The first visit downloads a large art and audio catalog. Keep the tab active and allow it to finish; later visits can use the browser cache. A hard refresh intentionally downloads more again.

    I cannot dig a tile.

    Stand next to a solid tile, aim with A/D/W/S and hold F, or hold primary mouse click over an adjacent tile. Some materials need repeated hits or a better-affinity pickaxe. UI overlays and tutorial locks can also pause gameplay input.

    I ran out of Gem Power underground.

    Land in a safe, lit place and release Flight and Quickslash. Lower torch intensity if needed; in Hardcore, keep light available to control stress while GP regenerates. Use a portal or the pause menu’s safe recovery tools when available.

    Where did my save go?

    Check that you are using the same browser profile and site address. Private windows, cleared site data and storage restrictions can separate local progress. Hardcore exhaustion alone does not auto-delete the save.

    Does the wiki work on a phone?

    Yes: navigation, search and the optional donation form are responsive. The current game beta remains keyboard-first, so a desktop browser provides the intended controls.

    How do I report a bug or incorrect wiki entry?

    Include the page or system name, what you expected, what happened and your browser. Send it to info@nextgen.run.
