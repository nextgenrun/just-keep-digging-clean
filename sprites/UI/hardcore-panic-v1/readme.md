# Hardcore Panic V1

ImageGen-authored escalation art for the canonical Hardcore stress bands.

- `panic-warning-medallion-v1.png` is the contained amber fracture shown from
  the warning threshold.
- `panic-critical-medallion-v1.png` is the split crimson-white medallion shown
  only at critical stress.
- `high-panic-edge-frame-v1.png` is the alpha-clean 16:9 danger edge frame. Its
  primary layer begins as barely visible peripheral unease before warning, a
  slow breathing copy joins through the middle range, and a faint displaced
  reality-slip copy appears only late. All layers keep the central gameplay
  region transparent.

Phaser supplies the live panic percentage, GP drain, and safety instruction.
The generated artwork contains no baked text and does not own gameplay state.
