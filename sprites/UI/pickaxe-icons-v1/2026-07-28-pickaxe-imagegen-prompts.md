# Pickaxe icon ImageGen prompt set

## Shared production direction

- Use case: `stylized-concept`.
- Asset type: production fantasy mining-game UI icon.
- Match the existing shop atlas's premium hand-painted materials, semi-realistic
  volume, warm/cool controlled rim lighting, crisp dark outline, generous
  padding, and readability at 34-64 px.
- Render exactly one complete pickaxe, centered and diagonal from lower-left
  handle to upper-right head, with no icon frame or badge.
- Use a perfectly flat removable chroma background with no shadow, gradient,
  texture, reflection, floor, particles, text, logo, or watermark.
- Make every tier structurally distinct; no recolor-only variants.

## Tier prompts

1. **Bronze:** compact hammered-bronze head with one pointed tooth and one
   stout chisel end, aged oak, dark leather wraps, brass rivets; sturdy,
   handmade, and entry-tier. Flat green chroma.
2. **Iron:** thick forged dark-iron head with long straight needle point and
   squared hammer/chisel poll, charcoal ash handle, iron collar, structural
   rivets, black grip; more engineered than Bronze. Flat green chroma.
3. **Steel:** sleek extended silver-steel head with swept piercing spike and
   broad sharpened adze, blued-steel spine plate, geometric collar, clean
   cross-bolted bands; precise and professionally manufactured. Flat green
   chroma.
4. **Mithril:** pale moon-silver asymmetric flowing crescents, openwork
   star-shaped collar, contained cyan rune channels, midnight-blue wrap,
   slender backbone, small aqua gemstone; magically light and rare. Flat green
   chroma.
5. **Adamant:** brutal black-green faceted head with thick beak, stepped
   cleaver, hexagonal armored hub, contained emerald seams, plated handle,
   broad dark grip, emerald cap; extremely dense and deep-earth forged. Flat
   magenta chroma.
6. **Rune:** cobalt-black ancient head around a round runestone hub, hooked
   raven-beak point, split crescent prongs, abstract contained violet rune
   grooves, rib-caged blackwood handle, indigo grip, violet crystal pommel.
   Flat green chroma.
7. **Dragon:** blackened dragon-skull hub with amber eye, long horn mining
   spike, red-black wing adze with serrated gold edge and talons, scale-armored
   handle, crimson grip, gold claw bands, tail pommel, contained magma seams.
   Flat green chroma.

Generation used the built-in ImageGen path. Bronze through Rune used the
existing atlas and/or the previous tier as a style-family reference where the
tool accepted the local reference; Dragon retained the same written family
contract after a transient reference-read failure.
