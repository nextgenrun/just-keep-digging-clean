## Gameplay direction: Unearthed starlight

The material should suggest stone being excavated to uncover celestial power.
Fresh fracture faces and subtle strata express digging; a few embedded cyan
crystal pockets express Star discovery; limited branching grain inside existing
bronze trim recalls Worldroot. The original word, broad letter shapes, bronze
border, floating fragments, underline, and four-point star anchor the identity.

This is a focused expression of mining and Star discovery, not a literal
illustration of every mechanic. Flight, merchants, and progression interfaces
are deliberately not represented by additional symbols in this draft.
