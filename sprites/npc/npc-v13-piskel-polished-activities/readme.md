Production pack containing the seven accepted stand-still activities for each
merchant. Every frame is chroma-audited, normalized with one fixed
per-merchant scale to match its calm baseline silhouette, anchored at
that merchant's measured calm-baseline lower-body root and foot position, and
round-tripped through an editable Piskel document.

No rejected quiet concepts, walking, roaming, per-frame scaling, or whole-body
wobble are present.
