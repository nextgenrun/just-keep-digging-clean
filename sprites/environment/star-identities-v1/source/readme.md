# Star Identity ImageGen Sources

These six PNG files are immutable built-in ImageGen source sheets for the
production `star-identities-v1` atlases.

## Final generation prompt contract

Every prompt requested a premium 2D fantasy game-asset contact sheet on pure
black, one isolated centered eight-point crystal Star per cell, generous safe
spacing, no text, no labels, no frames, no UI, no terrain, no characters, no
photography, no checkerboard, no cropped glow, and no repeated silhouette.
Each star needed a distinct crystal body plus a clearly different surrounding
light phenomenon so black could be converted to transparent alpha.

The six prompt variants were:

- **Common, 4 by 3:** Glacier Blue with snow dust; Cloud Cyan with vapor wings;
  Moonwhite with a moon bloom; Seafoam with tidal mist; Skyglass with glass
  rays; Frost Lilac with frost petals; Dawn Peach with dawn haze; Mint Whisper
  with orbiting motes; Rose Mist with a folded veil; Soft Amber with lantern
  haze; Periwinkle with feathered rays; Silver Rain with falling sparks.
- **Uncommon, 5 by 2:** Orchid Tide with tidal ribbons; Jade Pulse with leaf
  waves; Coral Veil; Electric Iris with lightning; Citrine Bloom with pollen;
  Aqua Lantern; Raspberry Halo with broken petals; Verdant Comet; Indigo Drift
  with nebula feathers; Copper Aurora with teal afterlight.
- **Rare, 5 by 2:** Solar Gold with prominences; Ruby Crown with ember arcs;
  Sapphire Flare; Emerald Nova with faceted dust; Amethyst Ray; Pearl Prism;
  Topaz Lightning; Rosegold Choir with harmonic waves; Scarlet Sun; Arctic
  Diamond with aurora dust.
- **Epic, 4 by 2:** Inferno Tangerine with coiling fire; Plasma Pink with fluid
  ribbons; Void Azure with a dark lens; Acid Lime with energetic vapor; Royal
  Ultraviolet with regal rays; Molten Crimson with liquid arcs; Storm Teal with
  cloud lightning; Eclipse Bronze with a corona.
- **Mythic, 3 by 2:** Dragon Jade with serpentine ribbons; Phoenix White with
  featherfire; Abyssal Turquoise with bioluminescent tendrils; Chrono Magenta
  with time echoes; Celestial Obsidian with prismatic edges and gravitational
  dust; Auric Ghost with ancestral flame wisps.
- **Astral, 2 by 2:** Eventide Spectrum with a full spectral aurora;
  Singularity Violet with gravitational lens arcs; Heavenfire Opal with
  prismatic flame; First-Light Prism with primordial ribbons.

The builder performs slicing, exterior-black alpha extraction, bounded
straight-alpha normalization, and manifest hashing. It does not invent or draw
replacement artwork.
