## Shared Production Prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production 2D Phaser parallax chamber card for a dark side-view mining game
Composition/framing: wide side-on orthographic game view; one complete harmless colossal creature filling roughly 65-72 percent of the canvas, fully inside frame with its defining silhouette readable; important head and torso detail in the central safe area; deep layered chamber with dark edges. No baked square foreground mine blocks because live terrain will mask the plate. No player, UI, labels, border, or vignette frame.
Style/medium: polished cinematic painterly realism matching premium UNDERSTAR scenic-v2 2D art; tactile geological materials; controlled localized emissive detail; strong readable values at gameplay scale.
Global constraints: preserve the exact creature identity from Image 1; harmless distant living scenery, not a boss; no generic redesign; no combat or attack pose; no arena; no health bar; no loot; no doors or traversable platforms; no text; no logo; no watermark; seamless finished production background card, not a concept sheet.
```
