# Titan Surface Stances v1

Production ImageGen cutouts shared by sharp underground Titans and the unlocked
surface Titan Walk.

- One independently generated stance exists for each of the 25 Titan identities.
- Runtime WebPs are transparent, baseline-aligned 768x768 images.
- Underground presentation reuses these full-resolution stances at chamber
  scale, and their alpha silhouettes author the exact covering-tile masks.
- High-resolution chamber cards remain subdued environmental context behind the
  stance and the compact underground dais.
- Every surface stance is grounded over the newer shared basalt dais with its
  own bounded size profile; the retained legacy Titan Walk plinth is no longer
  the live surface footing. No scenery, prop, pedestal, text, or Phaser-drawn
  substitute is baked into these creature assets.

Lossless chroma-key and alpha-clean ImageGen masters live in `sources/`. Exact
prompt provenance and the runtime inventory/decoded-image checks are recorded
by the dated manifests in this directory.
