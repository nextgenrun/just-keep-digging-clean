# Seedance Player and Effects Lab

Review-only workspace for the September 4, 2026 Seedance Mini player/effects
study. Generated clips, derived aftereffects, measured motion gates, and the
browser comparison sandbox stay isolated here until a separate production
approval and promotion step.

The hard budget is EUR 10. The generation runner refreshes current OpenRouter
model metadata before each paid batch and records reported cost per job. Raw
videos are never used directly as the gameplay player sprite.

Open the interactive review at
`http://127.0.0.1:8080/testing/animation-sandbox/2026-09-04-seedance-player-effects-eur10-v1/`.
It keeps the current town, player frame, and material sprites visible while the
selected VP9-alpha layer plays at the authored contact point. `PLAY ONCE` and
`INSPECT 3×` always run frames forward; repeats start from time zero only after
the prior one-shot has ended.

Current paid state: 21 clips completed, USD 2.9295 known cost (about EUR 2.5226
at the pinned 1.1615 reference rate), plus one submitted job awaiting a valid
key for polling. Six core effects are selected and alpha-verified. Player walk
and side-dig videos are retained as explicit rejections because they drift or
