## About This Game

**UNDERSTAR is a 2D action-progression mining adventure set between a living surface settlement and thousands of metres of dangerous underground strata.** Every descent is a choice: chase a rich ore seam, push into an unknown biome, hunt a constellation star, or risk going deeper before returning to the light.

### Carve your own route

Break through destructible terrain in the direction you choose. Read the rock, uncover ores and relics, chain productive digs into combos, and turn every expedition into permanent progress.

### Master movement and mining abilities

Flight-powered movement transforms vertical caverns into open routes. Quick Slash and Thunder Strike let you cut through obstacles and reshape how you explore the depths.

### Decode the constellations

Find stars hidden across the world and unlock constellation upgrades that change your abilities, movement, mining power, and survival underground.

### Build the Arc Core

Develop a spherical mining machine from a compact 2×2 rig into the immense Omega 8×8 form. Its growing footprint and power open routes that ordinary tools cannot.

### Survive a world that does not sit still

Day and night, rain and storms, underground darkness, earthquakes, cave-ins, distinct biomes, and dangerous depth all shape your expeditions. Your torch is not decoration—it is part of the plan.

### Return stronger

Trade with surface merchants, manage your resources, improve your equipment, and prepare for the next descent. The deeper you go, the more the world reveals.

**Dig below. Rise above. Discover what waits under the stars.**
