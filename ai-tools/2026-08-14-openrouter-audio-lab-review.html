1. Character voice model arena

Same scripts, different engines. Judge identity, emotional control, intelligibility, and whether the voice belongs in this underground world.

2. Experimental sound effects

General audio models are not a dedicated Foley service. These samples deliberately test that boundary and should be rejected if they contain speech, music, excess tail, or weak impact.

3. Existing voice-library intelligence

Two transcription models inspect the same current recordings. Agreement provides a foundation for subtitles, searchable dialogue, duplication checks, and localization.

4. Voice-clone consistency slot

Locked by design. The lab supports a future Fish Audio reference-voice comparison, but it will not send an existing performance until ownership and explicit voice consent are confirmed. A clean built-in voice identity remains the fallback.
