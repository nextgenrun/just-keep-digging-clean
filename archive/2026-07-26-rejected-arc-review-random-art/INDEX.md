# Rejected Arc Review Random Art

**Archived:** 2026-07-26

The user approved the Small/Omega Arc bodies, energy layers, dig effects,
cloud transitions, and foundry background, but explicitly rejected:

- the ornamental purple/gold HUD frame;
- the generated four-tile dirt/slate/floor/bedrock atlas;
- runtime tile crops, the tile Piskel project, and review mockups that showed
  those tiles;
- the older six-state browser contact sheet and captures that exposed visible
  HTML controls plus gray/brown placeholder blocks.

These files are provenance only. They are not loaded by Phaser and must not be
used as a world-art replacement or visual reference.

The approved replacement package is `sprites/vehicles/arc-core-v3/`, registered
through `values/arcCoreVisuals.sprite.json`.

## Recovery map

- `attachments/`: exact screenshots supplied with the rejection.
- `source/`: generated tile atlas source and keyed-alpha intermediate.
- `piskel/`: rejected tile-role Piskel document.
- `runtime/`: rejected tile crops.
- `preview/`: tile-based mockups and the legacy low-quality browser board.
- `tools/`: obsolete tile-based and browser-screenshot compositors.

Keep indefinitely as approval-boundary evidence.
