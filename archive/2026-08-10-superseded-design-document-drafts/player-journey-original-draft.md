The first 5 minutes of the game are the most important.


First 5 minutes >>> first 2 hours >>> all other gameplay.

This is because of the 2 hour refund window we want too polish the first 2 hours of active gameplay the most too prevent refunds, and hook the player so they keep playing and we have a high return rate.


----------------------------------
First 5 minutes

toturial is always optional, if player decides no toturial - ad with text are u sure u want too skip the toturial and figure out the game and its controlls by yourself? ''type yess'' popup where player has to type yes 

than player can chose gamemodes

Casual
relax dig more dig deeper gameplay experience no player loss no time pressure

hardcore (hardcore still need some form of time presure)
intended game experience where player constantly has to analyse risk reward and digging deeper brings bigger risk. 

Death is semi permanent permanent, player has 2 lifes on first death in normal hardcore always offer one free revive.

We want to revolve the game around this experience as the primairy experience but offer casual for a player that does not like permadeath.


A third hidden gamemode shall be added called one-life-hardcore wich actually has only one life
